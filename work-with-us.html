How we'll work together.

02

03

04

BEFORE WE TALK

A few useful things to prepare.

You don't need everything ready. Even a rough idea is enough to start.

About your project

  • What does your business or project do?
  • Who do you want the website to reach?
  • What should visitors do on the website?
  • Do you have an existing website or examples you like?

Practical details

  • Which pages or features do you think you'll need?
  • Do you already have a logo, text and images?
  • Is there a preferred launch date?
  • Do you have a budget range in mind?
