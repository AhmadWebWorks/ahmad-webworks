Privacy Notice.

How Ahmad WebWorks handles information you share through this website and our team application process.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Who we are

Ahmad WebWorks is an independent web studio operated by Ahmad Hassan Khan in Pakistan. For privacy questions or requests about your information, email ahmadhassankhan79@gmail.com.

2. Information you may provide

Depending on how you use the website, you may provide:

  • Contact details and project information when you make an enquiry
  • Name, email address, preferred role, location, skills, experience and portfolio link when applying to join the team
  • Profile details, professional links and project information if you become a team member and submit a profile

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or other sensitive personal information through website forms.

3. How information is used

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, understand project requirements, review team applications, communicate about interviews or onboarding, operate the team portal and maintain appropriate business records.

4. Service providers

Website application and team information is stored and processed using Supabase. Transactional application emails may be delivered using Brevo. These providers process information to provide their services. We do not sell personal information.

5. WhatsApp messages

When you submit a team application, your browser may open WhatsApp with a prepared message and application reference. The message is not sent automatically; you review it and choose whether to press Send. WhatsApp then handles that conversation under its own privacy terms.

6. Public team profiles

Team profile information and project details are not published automatically. A profile or project is displayed publicly only after the required review and approval process. Publicly displayed details may be viewed or copied by other internet users.

7. Retention and your choices

We keep information for as long as it is reasonably needed to respond to enquiries, review applications, operate the team system, maintain records or meet legal and security requirements. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion. Some records may need to be retained where a legal, security or operational reason applies.

8. Security

We use access controls and server-side processing to help protect information. No website or online storage system can guarantee absolute security, so please share only information relevant to your enquiry or application.

9. External websites

This website may link to WhatsApp, Facebook, GitHub or other third-party websites. Their services are governed by their own privacy notices and terms.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our website or processes change. The latest version and update date will appear on this page.
