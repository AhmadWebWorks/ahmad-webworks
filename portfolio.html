FEATURED LIVE PROJECT · CASE STUDY

Dua Boutique: a practical storefront journey.

A small-business website designed to bring product discovery, cart management and order enquiries into one straightforward customer journey.

PROJECT SCOPE

What was built

  • Separate Boutique and Cosmetics product sections
  • Product listings and a shopping cart
  • Checkout summary and Cash on Delivery order flow
  • WhatsApp handoff for customers to send order details
CUSTOMER JOURNEY

How it works

  1. Browse products by category
  2. Add selected items to the cart and adjust quantities
  3. Review the order at checkout
  4. Send the order details through WhatsApp for follow-up
BUILD APPROACH

Practical priorities

  • Keep the shopping flow understandable on mobile
  • Show quantities and totals before the order is sent
  • Keep the cart available while the order is being confirmed
TRANSPARENT STATUS

What we can verify

This is a live demonstration project. We describe the implemented features, but do not claim sales growth, conversion rates or customer outcomes without measured evidence.
